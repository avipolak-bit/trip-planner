Travel preferences

Flights on any of these airlines are left out. Codes: TK Turkish · MS EgyptAir · GF Gulf Air · QR Qatar · RJ Royal Jordanian · SV Saudia.

Preferred airlines are always shown first. Others appear only if they're quicker (1h+) or cheaper by at least the amount below — and quicker always ranks above cheaper. "Quicker" = total time from take-off at the origin to landing at the final destination, including layovers.

Low-cost airlines

Low-cost fares are compared including estimated bag & seat fees for your luggage. Self-transfers (separate tickets) need extra time to re-check in.

Connections

Flights with a connection or self-transfer longer than the maximum are left out of the results. Self-transfer = changing between separate tickets (usually low-cost airlines), where you collect bags and check in again.

Gett transfers

Used to suggest Gett pickup times once a flight is selected. Booking happens in your own Gett app — the planner never asks for your login.

Shabbat buffers

Flights inside the buffer aren't hidden — they're marked “Tight to Shabbat” so you can make an exception.

All tools in this planner are free: Kiwi.com live search, Google Flights, Skyscanner, Kayak, ITA Matrix, FlightConnections, FlightSeatmap seat maps and the ExpertFlyer free plan (1 seat alert). None of them needs a paid plan; if a site offers an upgrade, you can ignore it.

Flight results come straight from Kiwi.com (no AI suggestions). Airport data: OpenFlights (ODbL).

Shabbat and Yom Tov times follow Chabad.org's method — candles 18 min before sunset (or the city's Chabad custom), Shabbat ends when the sun is 8.5° below the horizon — calculated for the city centre, with Israel vs. diaspora holiday days handled. Expect ±1 min vs. Chabad.org; tap “Chabad.org” on any time to see their official listing. AI-suggested flights are based on typical schedules — always confirm the final itinerary and price via the booking links.
